## 一、英文原文

**The Paradox of Computational Power**

In an era where supercomputers perform quadrillions of calculations per second, it might seem that no problem lies beyond the reach of computation. Yet, as the mathematicians and computer scientists of the twentieth century discovered, there exist well-defined problems that no computer—regardless of its speed or memory capacity—can ever solve. This revelation, which emerged from the pioneering work of Alan Turing in the 1930s, fundamentally altered our understanding of what it means to compute and, by extension, what it means to know.

The theoretical foundation of these limitations rests on Turing's concept of a universal computing machine—an abstract device capable of simulating any algorithmic process. By demonstrating that certain problems, such as the famous "halting problem," cannot be decided by any algorithm, Turing established that there are inherent boundaries to mechanical reasoning. Building on this insight, researchers subsequently identified a vast hierarchy of computational complexity classes, most notably the distinction between problems that can be solved in polynomial time (P) and those for which solutions can merely be verified efficiently (NP). Whether P equals NP remains one of the deepest unsolved questions in all of mathematics, with profound implications for fields ranging from cryptography to optimization.

The practical consequences of these theoretical limits are far from academic. Modern encryption protocols, which safeguard everything from online banking to state secrets, derive their security from the widely-held but unproven assumption that certain mathematical operations are computationally intractable—that is, they cannot be reversed within any feasible timeframe, even with the most powerful computers imaginable. Should this assumption prove false, the very infrastructure of digital security would crumble overnight. Meanwhile, in the realm of artificial intelligence, the recognition that many real-world problems are computationally hard has spurred the development of heuristic and approximate methods that, while lacking the guarantees of exact algorithms, nevertheless yield remarkably useful results in practice.

What the study of computational limits ultimately teaches us is a lesson in intellectual humility. For all our technological ingenuity, there remain questions that resist definitive answers—not because we lack the tools or the talent, but because the very structure of logic and mathematics forbids them. In recognizing these boundaries, we gain not only a deeper appreciation for the nature of computation but also a clearer sense of where human intelligence, with its capacity for intuition and creativity, can complement the raw processing power of machines.

## 二、中文翻译

**计算能力的悖论**

在一个超级计算机每秒可执行千万亿次计算的时代，人们或许会认为没有任何问题超出了计算的能力范围。然而，正如二十世纪的数学家和计算机科学家所发现的，存在着明确定义的问题，任何计算机——无论其速度多快、内存多大——都永远无法解决。这一启示源自艾伦·图灵在 20 世纪 30 年代的开创性工作，从根本上改变了我们对「计算意味着什么」、进而对「认知意味着什么」的理解。

这些限制的理论基础建立在图灵的通用计算机概念之上——一种能够模拟任何算法过程的抽象装置。通过证明某些问题（如著名的「停机问题」）无法由任何算法判定，图灵确立了机械推理存在固有边界。在这一洞见的基础上，研究者们随后识别出了计算复杂性类的一个庞大层级，其中最引人注目的是：可在多项式时间内求解的问题（P 类）与解仅能被高效验证的问题（NP 类）之间的区分。P 是否等于 NP 至今仍是整个数学领域最深奥的未解难题之一，对从密码学到最优化的各个领域都有着深远影响。

这些理论极限的实际后果绝非纸上谈兵。现代加密协议保护着从网上银行到国家机密的一切，其安全性源自一个被广泛接受但未经证实的假设：某些数学运算在计算上是「难解的」——也就是说，即便动用可想象的最强大计算机，也无法在任何可行的时间框架内将其逆转。倘若这一假设被证伪，数字安全的整个基础设施将在一夜之间崩塌。与此同时，在人工智能领域，认识到许多现实世界问题在计算上的困难性，推动了启发式和近似方法的发展——这些方法虽缺乏精确算法的理论保证，却在实践中产生了极为有用的结果。

计算极限的研究最终教给我们的，是一种智识上的谦逊。尽管我们拥有种种技术巧思，仍有一些问题拒绝给出确定性的答案——不是因为我们缺乏工具或才智，而是因为逻辑和数学的内在结构本身禁止了这些答案。认识到这些边界，我们不仅对计算的本质有了更深的理解，也更清楚地看到了人类智能——以其直觉与创造力——如何能够补充机器的原始处理能力。

## 四、长难句解析

### 句 1 | 插入语 + 多重修饰

> **Yet, as the mathematicians and computer scientists of the twentieth century discovered, there exist well-defined problems that no computer—regardless of its speed or memory capacity—can ever solve.**

**句子骨架**：there exist problems

**结构拆解**：
```
[Yet], 
  └─ as [主语: mathematicians and computer scientists] discovered  ← 方式状语从句
[主干] there exist [主语: well-defined problems]
  └─ that [引导定语从句修饰 problems]
       └─ [主语: no computer] can ever solve
            └─ —regardless of its speed or memory capacity—  ← 插入语补充说明
```

**翻译技巧**：
- `as ... discovered` → 译为「正如…所发现的」，置于句首比原文更自然
- `there exist` 倒装句 → 中文保留「存在着」的无主语句式
- 插入语 `—regardless of...—` → 中文用破折号或括号保留补充语气

---

### 句 2 | 多重从句嵌套（定语 + 同位语 + 让步）

> **Modern encryption protocols, which safeguard everything from online banking to state secrets, derive their security from the widely-held but unproven assumption that certain mathematical operations are computationally intractable—that is, they cannot be reversed within any feasible timeframe, even with the most powerful computers imaginable.**

**句子骨架**：protocols derive their security from the assumption

**结构拆解**：
```
[主语: Modern encryption protocols]
  └─ , which safeguard everything (from A to B), ← 非限定性定语从句
[谓语: derive] [宾语: their security]
  └─ from [宾语: the assumption]
       ├─ widely-held but unproven  ← 并列形容词修饰
       ├─ that [同位语从句] certain operations are intractable
       └─ —that is, [解释性插入语]
            └─ they cannot be reversed
                 ├─ within any feasible timeframe
                 └─ , even with the most powerful computers imaginable
```

**翻译技巧**：
- 非限定性定语从句 `which safeguard...` → 中文前置为「保护着…的」过长，不如拆为并列分句
- `that is` → 译为「也就是说」保留解释语气
- `even with` → 译为「即便动用…」比直译「即便有」更生动

---

### 句 3 | 否定结构 + 并列因果

> **For all our technological ingenuity, there remain questions that resist definitive answers—not because we lack the tools or the talent, but because the very structure of logic and mathematics forbids them.**

**句子骨架**：there remain questions

**结构拆解**：
```
[让步状语: For all our ingenuity]  ← "for all" = despite
[主干: there remain questions]
  └─ that resist definitive answers  ← 定语从句
[原因状语]:
  └─ not because we lack [宾语: the tools or the talent]
  └─ , but because [主语: the structure] forbids [宾语: them]
       └─ very  ← 强调「正是」
       └─ of logic and mathematics
```

**翻译技巧**：
- `For all` → 「尽管」，表示让步，考研阅读高频表达
- `the very structure` → `very` 表强调，译为「内在结构本身」
- `not because ... but because ...` → 保持「不是因为…而是因为…」的排比节奏

## 五、翻译要点总结

| 技巧 | 示例 | 说明 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **长定语拆分** | `problems that ... can ever solve` → 「任何计算机…都无法解决的问题」 | 英文定语从句后置，中文前置太长的需断开 |
| **插入语处理** | `—regardless of ...—` → 保留破折号 | 英文插入语中文同样用破折号/括号 |
| **被动转主动** | `cannot be decided by any algorithm` → 「无法由任何算法判定」 | 「由」字句是中文被动的一种柔和表达 |
| **抽象名词具体化** | `intellectual humility` → 「智识上的谦逊」 | 加「上的」使抽象概念在中文中更自然 |
| **`that is` 翻译** | `that is, they cannot...` → 「也就是说，它们无法…」 | 保留口语化解说语气 |
| **`very` 表强调** | `the very structure` → 「内在结构本身」 | `本身` 比 `正是` 在科学语境中更自然 |

## 六、仿写练习

> 尝试用以下句式各写一个句子：

1. **For all + N, ...**（尽管…）
   > 例：For all its limitations, the algorithm remains remarkably efficient.

2. **not because ... but because ...**（不是因为…而是因为…）
   > 例：The project succeeded not because of luck, but because of meticulous planning.

3. **derive ... from ...**（从…中获得…）
   > 例：The theory derives its explanatory power from a single elegant principle.
